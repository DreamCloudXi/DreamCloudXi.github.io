Woven a meter at a time on a four-shaft floor loom, then cut, hemmed and finished with
a brass zip along the lower edge so the seam disappears when the cover is on.

The cotton is undyed, which means the color you see is the color the fibre grew. It
starts crisp and softens over the first few washes until it drapes properly.
