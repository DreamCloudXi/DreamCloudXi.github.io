Pinched from a single ball of terracotta and burnished with a river stone before
firing, which closes the surface enough to hold oil without any glaze at all. The
finish is a low sheen that comes from pressure rather than from a coating.

Fill it a third full with olive oil, lay a cotton wick in the spout with a centimetre
proud, and give it ten minutes to draw before lighting. It will burn for about four
hours on a filling.
