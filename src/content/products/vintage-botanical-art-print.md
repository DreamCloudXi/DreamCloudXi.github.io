The fern was pressed in a book for most of a winter, then scanned at a resolution high
enough to keep the veins. The image is separated into three screens — a pale ground, a
mid green and a near-black outline — and each is pulled by hand in turn.

Registration is done by eye, so on some sheets the outline sits a hair off the color
beneath it. We have stopped treating that as a fault.
