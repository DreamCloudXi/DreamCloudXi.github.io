Poured into the tumblers that came out of the kiln a shade off — the ones we would not
sell as cups, but which are perfectly good as a candle and perfectly good as a cup
again once the wax is gone.

The scent is cedar first, then fig leaf, then a thread of birch tar underneath that
reads as woodsmoke. Trim the wick to 5mm before each burn and the first hour will set
a full pool that stops it tunnelling.
