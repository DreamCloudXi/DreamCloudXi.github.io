Tell us the names, the date or the line you want, and one of us letters it by hand in
gouache. Nothing here is set in a typeface: the weight and the spacing are decided on
the page, which is why two frames carrying the same name never look quite the same.

We email a photograph of the finished lettering before it goes into the frame. If the
spelling is wrong or you have changed your mind, that is the moment to say so.
