Roughly four hundred knots, tied over two evenings. The rope is a three-ply undyed
cotton that frays into a soft fringe rather than a stringy one when it is combed out
at the bottom.

The dowel is driftwood, collected and dried rather than bought, so no two hangings are
the same width or hang at quite the same angle. It arrives folded and will want a day
on the wall to shake the creases out.
