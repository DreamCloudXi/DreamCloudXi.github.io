The beads are rolled from the offcuts of our own screen prints, which means the colors
in a strand are the colors of whatever was on the press that month. Each one is rolled
around a needle, glued, dried, then sealed in beeswax and shellac until it is hard
enough to knock against a table without marking.

Strung on waxed linen with a solid brass clasp and a short extender chain, so it sits
either at the collarbone or a little lower.
