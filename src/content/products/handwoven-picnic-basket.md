Willow is woven green, when it is still flexible enough to take a tight turn, then
left on the frame to dry into shape. What comes off a week later is stiff, light and
considerably stronger than it looks.

The liner is a heavy linen that lifts out for washing. The handle folds flat so the
basket can be stacked or slid under a bench, which is the part people say they did not
know they needed.
