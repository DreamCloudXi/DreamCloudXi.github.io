Every print in this series begins as a single unbroken line on tracing paper. The
drawing is exposed onto a silk screen and pulled by hand, one sheet at a time, which
is why no two are quite identical — a little more ink here, a fractionally heavier
edge there.

The paper is a 300gsm cotton rag with a deckled edge on all four sides. It takes the
ink deeply enough that the line has a slight relief you can feel with a fingertip.

Prints ship flat rather than rolled, in a rigid board mailer, so they arrive ready to
frame with no curl to flatten out first.
