The ash comes out of a joinery workshop two streets away, from boards too short for
their work and too good to burn. Each panel is carved from a single piece, so the
grain runs uninterrupted from one edge to the other and the knots fall where the tree
put them.

Finished in hardwax oil rather than varnish, which leaves the surface matte and open
enough to feel like wood rather than like a coating.
