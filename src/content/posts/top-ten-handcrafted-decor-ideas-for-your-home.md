## Start with the surfaces you touch

A hand-thrown mug, a wooden tray, a woven cushion. The things your hands meet every
day are where handmade work earns its place, because you notice the difference in
weight and finish long after you have stopped noticing the wall art.

## Then the walls

A macramé hanging over a plain wall, a single framed print at eye height, a small
group of ceramic plates. One considered piece does more than a crowded gallery.

## The list

1. A ceramic vase for whatever is in season
2. A woven basket for blankets or firewood
3. A hand-painted plate on the wall
4. A macramé hanging beside a window
5. A wooden panel above the sofa
6. A clay oil lamp on the sideboard
7. A framed botanical print in the hall
8. Beaded or knotted details on shelves
9. A scented candle poured in a real vessel
10. One thing you made yourself, however small

None of it needs to match. It only needs to have been made by someone who cared.
